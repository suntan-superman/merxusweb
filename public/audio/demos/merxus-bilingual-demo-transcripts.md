**Merxus AI:** Thank you for calling BrightPath Dental. I'm the Merxus AI assistant. How may I help you today?

**Elena:** Good morning. Are you accepting new patients? I would also like an appointment on Friday.

**Merxus AI:** Yes, we are accepting new patients. An appointment is available Friday at ten thirty. Would you like to request it?

**Merxus AI:** Perfect. I've recorded the request for Elena Ramirez. You will receive confirmation by text message.

**Caller:** And can you move my appointment to eleven?

**Merxus AI:** Yes. I've sent both requests to the office: Elena at ten thirty and your appointment at eleven. The team will confirm by text shortly.
